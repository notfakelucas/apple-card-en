Cookie Policy

This page describes the cookies and similar technologies we use on this website, what each one is for, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files saved by your browser when you visit a website. They let us remember preferences, identify your browser between visits, and help us understand how the site is used.

Beyond classic cookies, this site also uses localStorage and pixel identifiers, which we cover here for transparency.

2. Categories of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to work (session, in-progress order data, fraud prevention). No consent required.

  • applecard_lead — in-progress order data (localStorage).
  • tt_external_id — anonymous session identifier (localStorage).

Functional

Remember preferences (selected card color, shipping option).

Marketing

Used to measure and optimize our ad campaigns. Only activated after your explicit consent in the banner.

  • TikTok Pixel (_ttp) — TikTok campaign attribution, conversion measurement, and audience optimization. Sensitive data (email, phone) is hashed with SHA-256 before being sent.
  • Campaign parameters (tt_ttclid, tt_fbclid, tt_utm_*) stored in localStorage for conversion deduplication.

3. How to manage your preferences

On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject marketing cookies. You can change your mind at any time — just clear the site's cookies in your browser and the banner reappears.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies. More info:

  • Safari
  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Edge

4. Retention

Functional and marketing cookies are kept for 13 months after your last visit. localStorage identifiers are kept until you manually clear site data.
