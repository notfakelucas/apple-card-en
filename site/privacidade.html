Privacy Policy

This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you apply for Apple Card through this website. We process your data in accordance with applicable EU privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is the entity operating this website as an intermediary for the Apple Card product, issued by Goldman Sachs Bank Europe SE.

For any question about data processing, contact us through the support channel listed in section 8.

2. What data we collect

During the application process we collect the following categories of data:

  • Identification: full name, National ID Number, and date of birth.
  • Contact: email and mobile phone number.
  • Address: street, number, apt/suite, city, region, and postal code for physical card delivery.
  • Technical data: IP address, device identifier, user agent, campaign parameters (UTMs, ttclid), and navigation events.
  • Payment data: transaction ID, amount, and method. We do not store full card numbers — Cooud handles tokenization.

3. How we use your data

  • Process your Apple Card application and ship the physical card.
  • Verify your identity and meet regulatory requirements (anti-fraud, KYC).
  • Issue receipts and communications related to your order.
  • Optimize marketing campaigns via advertising platforms (see section 5).
  • Meet applicable legal and tax obligations.

4. Legal basis

Processing of your data is based on:

  • Contract performance — to process your application and payment.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax and anti-fraud requirements.
  • Consent — for marketing cookies and commercial communications.
  • Legitimate interest — for platform security and fraud prevention.

5. Sharing with third parties

Your data may be shared, on a strictly necessary basis, with:

  • Payment processor (Cooud) for card, bank transfer, and other payment processing.
  • TikTok Ads — pseudonymized conversion events (email and phone hashed with SHA-256) for campaign optimization, subject to your consent in the cookie banner.
  • Goldman Sachs Bank Europe SE and logistics partners (DHL, UPS, FedEx) for card issuance and delivery.
  • Public authorities when required by law.

6. Retention

We keep your data for as long as strictly necessary for the purposes above and for the minimum legal period applicable to financial transactions (generally up to 7 years for accounting records under EU tax law).

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us through the channel below.

Under the GDPR, you also have the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe your data has been processed unlawfully.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, email us at privacy@apple-card-en.vercel.app.
