Frequently asked questions
Why am I only paying shipping?
The Apple Card is 100% free with no annual fee. You're only paying the shipping cost for the physical titanium card to your address, with secure packaging and tracking.
When will I receive my card?
Delivery time depends on the option you chose: Standard (7-10 days), Express (4-5 days), or Premium (1-2 days). You'll receive tracking by email and SMS.
Is the payment secure?
Yes. Payments are processed by Cooud over PCI-compliant infrastructure with 256-bit SSL encryption. We never see your card number — Cooud tokenizes it before processing.
Can I cancel my order?
You can request cancellation up to 14 days after payment with a full refund. Just contact support via iMessage or email.
